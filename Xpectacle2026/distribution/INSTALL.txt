Xpectacle 2.0.0 — Golden Gate compatibility preview

1. Drag Xpectacle.app to Applications.
2. Open Xpectacle from Applications.
3. This preview is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. macOS may block
   the first launch. If you trust this release, use System Settings > Privacy &
   Security > Open Anyway after attempting to open it.
4. Grant Xpectacle access in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
   No Screen Recording permission is required.
5. Use the menu bar grid icon to open Settings and configure shortcuts and snapping.

Requires macOS 14 or newer. Includes native Apple Silicon and Intel binaries.
Quit Spectacle or other window managers if their shortcuts conflict.

Updates: https://github.com/lammworks/Xpectacle/releases
Source and license: https://github.com/lammworks/Xpectacle

This preview has no automatic updater. Its Settings/Updates link opens GitHub.
